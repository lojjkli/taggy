6. Tickets and messaging

Opening a ticket starts a conversation with TAGGY in DMs and creates a private staff channel in the relevant server. Messages, questionnaire answers and supported attachments are relayed between you and that server's ticket staff. The conversation is available to authorized staff and TAGGY's operator as described in the Privacy Policy.

Authorized dashboard users and staff can send messages as TAGGY. Those messages are delivered by the bot account. File, image, GIF and video delivery depends on Discord's permissions and limits; where website uploads are unavailable, use Discord's ticket channel or bot DM conversation.

Closing a ticket can generate a staff transcript. Administrators can delete closed ticket channels and customize deletion reminders. Closing or deleting a channel does not automatically erase every saved answer, log, DM or staff copy. See the retention details and contact us for a data deletion request.

Delivery and replies depend on user DM settings, channel access and service availability. TAGGY is not an emergency service and does not guarantee a staff response time. Staff applications do not guarantee acceptance or any paid position.
