Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Taggy ("Taggy", "the Bot", "we", "us", or "our") accesses, uses, stores, and shares information when Taggy is used on Discord and for Roblox account verification. Taggy is operated from Switzerland. Questions and privacy requests can be sent to zzzzzworks@gmail.com.

1. Information Taggy Accesses or Processes

Depending on the feature being used and the permissions granted to Taggy, the Bot may access or process:

  • Discord identifiers, such as user IDs, server IDs, channel IDs, message IDs, and role IDs.
  • Basic Discord profile information needed for features such as user information, avatar display, player lookup, moderation, ranks, and teams, including usernames, display names, avatars, and server roles.
  • Server and channel information needed to perform moderation and configuration commands.
  • Commands, interactions, and message-event information needed to respond to commands or operate moderation features. Text supplied to a command may be processed temporarily so Taggy can perform the requested action.
  • Giveaway information, such as giveaway identifiers, timing, entrants or winner identifiers where needed, and active giveaway state.
  • When you choose Roblox verification, your Roblox user ID, username, and display name. The "Verify with Roblox" option uses the openid and profile permissions to obtain this basic identity information after you approve the Roblox consent screen. The profile-code and in-game hub methods also use basic Roblox identity information to verify your account.
  • Messages and attachment names or links in support tickets when staff provide assistance or a ticket is closed.

2. Information Taggy Stores

Taggy may persistently store the following information when required for its features:

  • Player ranks and team assignments for a Discord server.
  • Active giveaway data.
  • Trap channel IDs.
  • Configured administrator and member role IDs.
  • Discord identifiers associated with the items above when needed for the feature to work.
  • Roblox and Discord account identifiers and Roblox usernames or display names needed to maintain an account link and the Member role.
  • Ticket ownership, status, staff actions, close reasons, and transcript files in a staff-only Discord logs channel.

Roblox OAuth access tokens are used to complete verification and are not stored as account links. Taggy does not intentionally request or collect passwords, Discord account tokens, payment-card information, home addresses, or other sensitive credentials.

3. How We Use Information

We use information only as reasonably necessary to provide, secure, maintain, and troubleshoot Taggy's stated features, including moderation, server configuration, ranks and teams, giveaways, trap channels, information commands, support tickets, Roblox-to-Discord account linking, Member role assignment, and responding to user interactions.

4. Legal Basis Where Applicable

Where applicable law requires a legal basis for processing, we process information as necessary to provide the features requested by users or server administrators, for legitimate interests such as operating and securing Taggy, or with consent where consent is required.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell Discord API data or personal information. Information may be shared only:

  • With Discord as necessary to use Discord's platform and APIs.
  • With Roblox as necessary to run the authorization flow and obtain the basic identity information you approve.
  • With authorized server staff through private ticket channels and staff-only logs, including transcript files created when tickets close.
  • With service providers used to host, secure, or operate Taggy, only as needed to provide those services.
  • When required by applicable law or a valid legal process.
  • When a user or authorized server administrator expressly directs Taggy to perform an action that requires sharing information within Discord.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain stored information only for as long as reasonably necessary for Taggy's stated functionality, security, or legal obligations. Active giveaway data may be removed after the giveaway no longer requires it. Server configuration, rank, team, and moderation configuration data may be retained while needed for that server. Roblox-to-Discord account links are retained while verification is active or until the link is removed. Ticket transcript files posted to the staff-only logs channel are scheduled for deletion after 30 days. Transcript files contain message text, embed text, and attachment names or links, but do not copy the attachment files. The original ticket channel and its conversation are not automatically deleted when the transcript expires and may remain until authorized staff remove them.

Users may request deletion of personal data linked to their Discord account, and server owners or authorized administrators may request deletion of stored server data, by emailing zzzzzworks@gmail.com. We may ask for reasonable verification before acting on a request. We will delete data when it is no longer necessary for Taggy's stated functionality or when deletion is otherwise required.

7. International Processing

Taggy is operated from Switzerland. Discord and service providers used to operate Taggy may process information in other countries. Where required by applicable law, appropriate safeguards will be used for international transfers.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal data, or to object to or restrict certain processing. To make a request, contact zzzzzworks@gmail.com.

9. Children's Privacy

Taggy is intended only for people who are permitted to use Discord. Users must meet Discord's minimum age requirement, which is at least 13 and may be higher depending on the user's country. We do not knowingly seek to collect data from anyone who is below the minimum age required to use Discord.

10. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect information handled by Taggy. No online service can guarantee absolute security. If we become aware of unauthorized access requiring notification under applicable law or Discord's rules, we will take appropriate steps.

11. Discord

Taggy is a third-party application and is not operated or endorsed by Discord or Roblox. Use of those platforms is also governed by their own terms and privacy policies.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Taggy's features, data practices, or legal obligations change. The updated version will be posted here with a revised "Last updated" date.

13. Contact

Privacy questions, deletion requests, and other data requests can be sent to zzzzzworks@gmail.com.
